A message from Jeremy

Thank you for making this possible.

Friends, family, and partners: since August, I’ve stepped into full-time ministry with FCA in Cherokee County. Every Friday night, every classroom, and every weight room I get to walk into is there because you prayed, gave, and believed in this.

Here is what the last two months have looked like. Team meals and prayer at Creekview. Practices, weight rooms, and game nights with the Knights at The King’s Academy. And every Tuesday morning, a weekly discipleship huddle with the wrestling team at Cherokee High School.

The photo here is me preparing to lead. It’s also my Thursday night Bible study. God challenged me to step out in boldness and start it myself. It meets every Thursday at 7:30, it’s open to anyone, and some of the students I’ve met in the schools have started coming. You can join online here.

One of the best parts has been watching students get curious. They look up to me, and they ask hard questions. One guy asked me a really hard one, and it meant a lot to see how badly he wants to challenge himself in following the Lord.

The moment that hit me hardest came at Creekview. A football player stood up in front of everyone and shared his testimony on identity. An injury got him cut, and he was out for a year. In that season he realized he had put his identity in sports, and that you can’t build it on anything in this world. That is my story. I grew up in church and still lost myself, and at 23 Jesus found me. Hearing a student say it out loud was the coolest thing I’ve seen this year.

What’s ahead excites me even more than what’s behind. Thank you for standing with me.

— Jeremy

Stories of impact

More than a moment.

Creekview High School

“Identity.”

A football player at Creekview stood up in front of the whole team and shared his testimony. An injury got him cut, and he was out for a year. He realized he had put his identity in sports, and that identity can’t rest on anything in this world.

The guys responded so well. Every one of them was affected. It was my story, told by a student, and I was so happy to watch it happen.

Friday nights · The King’s Academy

Back on the team.

At King’s Academy I started practicing with the team and going into the weight room with them. On Friday nights I got to feel like a football player again, just being part of the team.

I also got to pray over the coaches and pray with the team. It’s a Christian school, and it still matters to have someone in the huddle pointing the team to Jesus.

Cherokee High School · Tuesday mornings

A seat at the table.

Every Tuesday morning I meet with the Cherokee High School wrestling team for a weekly discipleship huddle. Chick-fil-A foil wraps, a classroom full of wrestlers, and an environment where we are learning how to follow Jesus.

It’s simple, and it’s where trust gets built.

The bigger picture

Why we show up.

Coaches and athletes carry enormous influence on their teams, their schools, and their communities. Reach one of them with the gospel, and you reach everyone they influence.

Vision

“To see the world transformed by Jesus Christ through the influence of coaches and athletes.”

Mission

“To lead every coach and athlete into a growing relationship with Jesus Christ and His church.”

Source: fca.org/what-is-fca and fca.org/about

My part is simple: be present, build relationships, and disciple the ones God opens up, so they can make disciples. Not Christians who attend. Disciples who multiply.
